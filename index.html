Ability Stack Inc. — Deep Dive

ENTERPRISE WORKFORCE UNIFICATION

Replacing Fragmented HR Tools with an AI-Powered Operating System

Enterprises struggle with fragmented software stacks: core salaried employees live in legacy HRIS tools, shift workers are managed via spreadsheets, and external contractors are routed through disjointed vendor portals. Ability Stack is built to unify the entire extended workforce into a single intelligent platform.

Core Modules Spearheaded:
  • ✓ SoW-based Service Procurement System: Automates contract generation, milestone tracking, and milestone-based approvals for enterprise vendors.
  • ✓ Shift Management Suite: AI-driven roster generation factoring in worker certifications, overtime constraints, and demand forecasting.
  • ✓ Global Employer of Record (EOR) Workflows: Built-in multi-country compliance checks, tax withholding algorithms, and onboarding automation.
  • ✓ Low-Code Configuration & API Architecture: Enabled enterprise customers in healthcare and staffing to integrate in under 2 weeks without custom code re-writes.

Research Publication Abstract

IJSRD · 2020

Open Innovation based Gamified Learning Framework for Corporates to up-skill Student’s Employability Index

Authors: Vivek Shivaswamy et al. · International Journal of Scientific Research and Engineering Development
Abstract: The traditional pipeline connecting higher education institutions and corporate talent acquisition is fraught with skill mismatches. This paper proposes a novel framework centered on open innovation, where enterprises sponsor real-world challenges through gamified learning loops. By tracking students’ iterative code submissions, collaboration scores, and problem structuring, the system dynamically computes an objective Employability Index that correlates higher with on-the-job productivity than conventional academic grading.

Key PM Takeaway: Built early foundations for Vivek's later ventures in talent orchestration and skill verification systems at Demlea and Ability Stack.

IEEE Publication Abstract

IEEE · 2018

Towards Solving the IoT Standards Gap

Authors: Vivek Shivaswamy · Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE)
Abstract: Despite the exponential proliferation of smart home and industrial IoT sensors, fragmented proprietary protocols (Zigbee, Z-Wave, proprietary Bluetooth profiles, RESTful clouds) inhibit seamless cross-device interoperation. This paper evaluates the Open Connectivity Foundation (OCF) standard as a vendor-neutral protocol bridging IP and non-IP networks. It models latency, security handshakes, and developer ergonomics, illustrating how open interoperability architectures drastically reduce consumer onboarding drop-offs.

Context: Authored during Vivek's 6-year tenure at Samsung Electronics as Ecosystem Evangelist at OCF.
